Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 July 2026

Ember does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. Everything the app records stays on your device.

What is stored on your device

Ember saves the following locally, in the app's own storage. It is never uploaded anywhere, and no one but you can read it:

  • Your focus session history — length, date, outcome, and the theme used
  • Your current streak and daily session count
  • Your selected theme and bubble timer preferences
  • Which themes you have unlocked

Deleting Ember from your device permanently erases all of it.

Purchases

Ember offers optional one-time purchases to unlock cosmetic themes. These are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Ember receives only a confirmation from Apple that a purchase completed, which it uses to unlock the theme on your device. Payment details are never seen by, or shared with, this app or its developer. Apple's handling of that transaction is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

The "Restore Purchases" button asks Apple which items your Apple Account already owns, so they can be unlocked again on a new device or after reinstalling.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Ember schedules them locally on your device to tell you a focus session has finished. There is no server involved and no push notifications are sent. You can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings.

Live Activities and widgets

Ember can show a running session on your Lock Screen, in the Dynamic Island, and in a Home Screen widget. This information is generated and displayed entirely on your device.

Focus guard

While a session is running, Ember detects whether you have switched to another app or simply locked your phone, so that locking your phone doesn't put the flame out. Ember cannot see which app you switched to, and this detection is not recorded or transmitted.

Third parties

Ember contains no advertising networks, no analytics services, and no third-party software development kits that collect data. The only external party involved is Apple, and only for processing purchases.

Children

Ember is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Any change that affects how data is handled will be reflected here before the relevant app update is released.
