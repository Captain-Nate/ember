Support

Common questions, and how to reach a human.

Email

Anything not answered below — emberfocustimer@gmail.com. Ember is made by one person, so replies usually take a day or two. If you're reporting a bug, your iPhone model and iOS version help a lot.

Sessions and the flame

My flame went out and I didn't quit

Ember puts the flame out if you leave for another app for more than about ten seconds while a session is running. That's the whole point of it — it's what makes finishing mean something.

Locking your phone is always safe. So is a call or a notification you don't tap.

Does the timer keep running when my phone is locked?

Yes. Lock your phone and the session keeps going. You'll see it on the Lock Screen, and in the Dynamic Island if your iPhone has one. When it's done, you'll get a notification and a chime.

I closed the app and lost my session

Force-quitting Ember ends the running session. Leave it in the background or lock the phone instead.

Sound and notifications

I didn't hear the chime

Ember respects your ring/silent switch and any Focus mode you have on, so it stays quiet when your phone is set to be quiet. Flip the switch on the side of your iPhone and check that Ember is allowed to notify you in Settings › Notifications › Ember.

No notification when a session finishes

Open Settings › Notifications › Ember and make sure notifications are allowed. If you declined the permission when you first opened the app, this is where to turn it back on.

Themes and purchases

How do I get my themes back?

Open the shop and tap Restore. That asks Apple which themes your Apple Account already owns and unlocks them again. Use the same Apple Account you bought them with.

I bought the bundle — do I get future themes?

Yes. The all-themes bundle includes every theme in Ember now and every theme added later, at no extra cost.

I paid and nothing unlocked

Try Restore first — that fixes almost every case. If it doesn't:

  1. Check you're signed into the Apple Account you purchased with
  2. Make sure you're online, then reopen Ember
  3. Still stuck? Email us with the date of the purchase and we'll sort it out

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

How do I delete everything?

Delete Ember from your device. All of it — sessions, streak, candles, settings — is stored only on your phone, so removing the app removes the data. Purchases stay attached to your Apple Account and can be restored.

Ember collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere. See the privacy policy.
